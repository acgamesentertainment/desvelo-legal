DESVELO

Deleting your Desvelo data

Desvelo, by Coleman99 on Google Play, creates no accounts of its own and has no servers: your progress lives on your phone and, if you sign in to Google Play Games, in a copy saved in your Google account. You can delete both whenever you want.

From the game

  • Only the cloud copy: Settings → Account & cloud → “Delete my cloud data”. It is deleted at once and your other phones stop uploading their progress.
  • Everything: Settings → Privacy → “Delete all my progress”. It deletes the progress on the phone and, if you are signed in, the cloud copy too.

Without opening or installing the game

  • Cloud copy: go to your Google Play Games profile (play.google.com/games/profile or the Play Games app), find Desvelo and choose to delete the game's data. You can also manage it from your Google Account (myaccount.google.com).
  • Data on the phone: uninstalling Desvelo deletes it.
  • If you have any trouble, or want us to delete a report you sent, write to acgamesentertainment@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my Desvelo data”. We reply within 30 days at most.

What is deleted and what is kept

Deleted: your progress, Codex, campaign, Refuge, unfinished match, error log and the cloud copy. Your settings (sound, controls, accessibility) stay on the phone until you uninstall the game.

Kept: the purchase records Google Play keeps, which let you recover your purchases if you reinstall the game. The developer keeps no data of yours on any server.
