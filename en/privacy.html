Desvelo Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026. Responsible party: Andy Enrique Cole Sáenz, developer of Desvelo on Google Play under the name Coleman99. Contact: acgamesentertainment@gmail.com.

Desvelo is a game that works without the internet and without creating accounts. It shows no ads, uses no analytics or advertising tools and has no servers of its own.

What is stored and where

  • On your phone: your progress (level, Codex, campaign, Refuge, recent matches and errands), your settings, any unfinished match and a log of the game's latest errors. It never leaves your phone except in the cases below.
  • In your Google Play Games account, only if you sign in (it is optional): Google gives the game your Play Games display name and stores a copy of your progress in its saved games service, so you can recover it on another phone. Google keeps that copy in your account; the developer has no access to it. Google handles that data under its own privacy policy.
  • Purchases: Google Play processes them. The game only receives which item you bought and its order number, so it can deliver it; this is stored with your progress. We never see your payment details.
  • Problem reports: if you use “Report a problem”, you decide whether and how to send it (by email or by copying it). Before sending, you see everything it includes: what you write, the version, the phone model, settings, a summary of your progress and the logged errors. It is only used to fix bugs and is deleted whenever you ask.

Local multiplayer

  • If you create or join a room, the game connects directly to the nearby phones in that room over Bluetooth and Wi‑Fi (Nearby Connections, part of Google Play services), without the internet and without going through any server.
  • Only the name you type for the room, your gear (tools, companion and abilities), the laws you already know and your moves during the match travel between those phones. Nobody stores them: they are gone when the room closes.
  • To find nearby phones, Android asks for the “Nearby devices” permission (and, on Android 12L or earlier, location, because the system requires it to scan over Bluetooth). The game does not use your location for anything else and only asks for these permissions when you create or join a room.

What we don't do

We don't ask for your real name, email, location, contacts, photos or any other personal data (in local multiplayer you can use any nickname). We don't share or sell data. There is no advertising and no cross-app tracking.

Google Play may collect crash reports for the app on its own if you agreed on your phone to share usage and diagnostics data with Google; Google handles that under its own policy.

Deleting your data

  • Cloud data: in the game, Settings → Account & cloud → “Delete my cloud data”.
  • Everything: Settings → Privacy → “Delete all my progress” (it also deletes your cloud copy if you are signed in).
  • Without opening the game: you can delete the game's data from your Google Play Games profile, and uninstalling it deletes what is stored on the phone.
  • Reports you sent us: write to acgamesentertainment@gmail.com and we will delete them.

Children

The game is intended for people aged 13 and over and collects no personal data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this same page with the new date. If a change affects what data is collected, the game will tell you first.
